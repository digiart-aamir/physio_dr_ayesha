Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you visit patients at home?

Yes — Dr. Ayesha offers both clinic visits at Prime Care and home visits across Rawalpindi and Islamabad.

Is hijama (cupping) safe?

Yes, when performed by a trained practitioner with sterile, single-use equipment — exactly how every hijama session at Prime Care is done.

How long does a session take?

Most physiotherapy and hijama sessions run 45–60 minutes; cardiac rehab sessions are scheduled weekly and reviewed monthly.

Which areas do you cover?

Rawalpindi and Islamabad, including home visits — message us your area to confirm availability.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any previous reports, scans or prescriptions related to your condition, and comfortable clothing you can move in.
